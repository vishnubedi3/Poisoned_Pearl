# Rules of the Universe

## Source Material

### Patch 1 (Historical)
The universe functions according to rules. The fact that something is undefined does not mean it lacks an explanation. This principle applies to the Great Awakening as well: the absence of a discovered cause for the formation of the universe does not imply that no explanation exists.

### Patch 2 (Current Established Version)
The universe functions according to rules. The absence of a discovered explanation does not mean that no explanation exists. This principle applies to the Great Awakening and to other phenomena whose causes remain unknown.

## Organizational Metadata & Relationships

- **Entity Type:** Fundamental Law / Concept
- **Core Principle:** The universe functions according to rules. Phenomena with undiscovered causes still possess underlying explanations.
- **Lore Evolution:** Patch 2 broadens the application of this rule from just the Great Awakening to explicitly include "other phenomena whose causes remain unknown."
- **Cross-References:**

  - [Multiplicity of Universes](../cosmology/multiplicity_of_universes.md): The mechanisms governing multiple universes are explicitly stated to "remain unknown," making this another phenomenon governed by the Rules of the Universe (i.e., an explanation must exist).
