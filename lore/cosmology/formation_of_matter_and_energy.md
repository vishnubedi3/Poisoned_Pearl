# Formation of Matter and Energy

## Source Material

After the Great Awakening, matter and energy began forming and accumulating according to the rules of the universe.

## Organizational Metadata & Relationships

- **Entity Type:** Cosmological Process
- **Chronology:** Occurred strictly *after* the Great Awakening.
- **Governing Law:** The formation and accumulation of matter and energy are governed by the established [Rules of the Universe](../concepts/rules_of_the_universe.md).
