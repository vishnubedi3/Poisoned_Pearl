# The Great Awakening

## Source Material

### Patch 1 (Historical)
The universe was originally completely empty. The Great Awakening was the event that awakened the universe. After this event, the formation of the universe began.

No event occurring at the moment of the Great Awakening has been discovered as the cause of the subsequent formation of the universe. If such a cause exists, it remains undiscovered.

### Patch 2 (Current Established Version)
The Great Awakening marked the beginning of the universe’s formation. Before it, the universe was completely empty. No cause for the Great Awakening has been discovered. If a cause exists, it remains unknown.

## Organizational Metadata & Relationships

- **Entity Type:** Cosmological Event
- **Significance:** The instigating event that transitioned the universe from a "completely empty" state to the beginning of its formation.
- **Established Unknowns:** No cause for the Great Awakening has been discovered. If a cause exists, it remains unknown.
- **Lore Evolution:** Patch 2 refines the phrasing of the event, reinforcing that the universe was completely empty before it, and streamlining the assertion that its cause remains undiscovered.
